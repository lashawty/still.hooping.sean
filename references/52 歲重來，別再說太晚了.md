# 52 歲重來，別再說太晚了

- 網址：https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXIzkLjDS5q/
- 帳號：@melrobbins（Mel Robbins）
- 發布：2026-04-15｜長度：107 秒｜按讚 3.6 萬｜留言 1515（2026-11-02 抓取）
- 形式：室內對鏡頭講話，開頭一張標題卡「Stop Saying "It's Too Late"」，之後是逐句大字幕，字幕和口白同步。
- 文案（節錄）：It's not too late. You are not too old. You are not behind. My life looked pretty different 5 years ago.……

## 口白（原文）

> It's your friend Mel, and I don't ever want to hear you say that you're too late, or too old, or too young, or too whatever to change your life for the better, or to go after the things that you want, or to reinvent your life.
>
> Most of you don't realize that just five years ago I was having to figure out what I was going to do next in my career. There was no Mel Robbins podcast. There was no Let Them Theory book. My talk show had been canceled. I had moved to a brand new place. I was starting over at 52. I didn't know what I was going to do next.
>
> In fact, think about this. Most of you only know me because of the things that I've done in the last three and a half years, in my mid to late fifties. That's right. I started the Mel Robbins podcast here in my closet in Southern Vermont. Over three and a half years, we have grown to the third largest podcast in the world. We now have 50 full-time people that work at our studios in Boston. It blows my mind what I have created. The Let Them Theory book, we only put that out last year in December. That book has only been published and in the world for just over a year.
>
> You have no idea what you're capable of, but you will never experience what you're capable of if you keep making up excuses and you don't get started. You are capable of creating extraordinary things, of reinventing your life, of blowing your own mind. I certainly have blown my mind in terms of what has happened and what we've created, but you will never experience it if you don't get started.
>
> So stop with the excuses, and just define what you want and get started. Get to work. And I promise you, you will just be blown away by the things that you are able to change in your life and by the things that you can create. And just know that your friend Mel Robbins, I'm going to be cheering for you every step of the way.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 我是你的朋友 Mel。我再也不想聽到你說，你太晚了、太老了、太年輕了、太什麼了，所以沒辦法把人生變好、沒辦法去追你想要的東西、沒辦法重新打造你的人生。
>
> 你們大多數人不知道，就在五年前，我還在想職涯下一步要做什麼。那時候沒有 Mel Robbins podcast，沒有《Let Them Theory》這本書，我的脫口秀被砍了，我搬到一個全新的地方。我 52 歲從頭開始，不知道接下來要做什麼。
>
> 說真的，你們想想：你們大多數人認識我，都是因為我這三年半做的事，而那是在我五十五歲到五十九歲之間。沒錯。Mel Robbins podcast 是在佛蒙特州南部、我家的衣帽間裡開始的。三年半下來，我們成了全世界第三大的 podcast。現在波士頓的工作室有 50 個全職員工。我自己做出來的東西，連我自己都不敢相信。《Let Them Theory》這本書去年 12 月才出版，到現在也才一年多。
>
> 你根本不知道自己能做到什麼。但如果你一直找藉口、不開始，你就永遠不會體驗到自己能做到什麼。你有能力創造了不起的事，有能力重新打造人生，有能力讓自己都嚇一跳。我自己就被這幾年發生的事、做出來的東西嚇到了。但你不開始，就永遠體驗不到。
>
> 所以別再找藉口，想清楚你要什麼，然後開始。動手做。我跟你保證，你會被自己能改變的事、能創造的東西震撼到。然後記得，你的朋友 Mel Robbins 會在每一步替你加油。
