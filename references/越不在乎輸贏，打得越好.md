- 文案：Detach yourself from the outcome

## 口白（原文）

> The game gets more fun the less it matters, and the less it matters, the better you perform. Because the game rewards the one who seeks no reward but the game itself.
>
> If you're doing something primarily because you want to win or you want money, then your brain is wired to seek that thing and that thing alone. Inevitably the day will come where you're not winning, or you're not making as much money, and when it does, you're going to begin to hate the task that's required to get it.
>
> But the person who loves the game for the sake of the game can never be disappointed, because they're not dependent on an outcome. The game is the one constant. So if you let go and enjoy the game for what it is, you will have a much more fun life.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 越不在乎這場比賽，打起來越好玩；越不在乎，打得越好。因為比賽獎勵的是那個不求回報、只求比賽本身的人。
>
> 如果你做一件事主要是為了贏，或是為了錢，你的大腦就會被設定成只追那個東西。總有一天你會不再贏，或是賺得沒那麼多，到那時候，你會開始討厭為了得到它而必須做的事。
>
> 但是為了比賽本身而愛比賽的人，永遠不會失望，因為他們不靠結果。比賽是唯一不變的東西。所以如果你放開，單純享受比賽本來的樣子，你的人生會好玩很多。
