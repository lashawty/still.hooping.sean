# 你怎麼可能在自己的旅程上遲到

- 網址：https://www.instagram.com/reel/C7poQk7udGw/
- 帳號：@mindbullypodcast（Mind Bully｜Podcast + Community）
- 發布：2024-05-31｜長度：35 秒｜按讚 25.9 萬｜留言 2010（2026-11-02 抓取）
- 形式：戶外橋上側拍，樹林背景，紅色全大寫逐句字幕，字幕和口白同步。
- 文案（節錄）：you're not too late / you're not too old / you're right on time / you haven't missed it……SAVE THIS!

## 口白（原文）

> You ain't late. You haven't missed it. And what is there to miss? You haven't missed it. You're not late. This is your journey. This is your story. How can you be late to your own journey, to your own party? You're not late. This is literally par for the course. This has been designed this way for a reason. Every single step that you've taken to get to this point, it's for a reason.
>
> You don't have to trust it. When you place your faith in so many other things, you place so much faith in you losing, so much faith in you not getting to where you want to be, so much faith in their opinion. But you don't put faith in your walk, in your journey, in your steps. Keep on stepping.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 你沒有遲到。你沒有錯過什麼。到底有什麼好錯過的？你沒有錯過。你沒有遲到。這是你的旅程，這是你的故事。你怎麼可能在自己的旅程上遲到、在自己的派對上遲到？你沒有遲到。這根本就是這條路本來的樣子。它被設計成這樣是有原因的。你走到這裡的每一步，都是有原因的。
>
> 你可以不相信。你把信心放在那麼多別的東西上：那麼相信自己會輸、那麼相信自己到不了想去的地方、那麼相信別人的意見。卻不相信自己走的路、自己的旅程、自己的腳步。繼續走下去。
