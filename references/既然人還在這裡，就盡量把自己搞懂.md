# 既然人還在這裡，就盡量把自己搞懂

- 發布：2025-06-30｜長度：71 秒｜按讚 1.4 萬｜留言 292（2026-11-02 抓取）

- 文案：While you're here, you might as well learn you. It's never too late.

## 口白（原文）

> Hear me when I say this, please, and thank you. It is never too late to shift your mindset and your frequency. They say an old dog can't learn new tricks. That's a lie. An old dog can learn new tricks, as long as you are persistent, you are intentional, you are resilient, you have grit, you are willing to do what's difficult and go back in parts of your life that you've probably been sweeping under the rug.
>
> I've seen people who are 60, 70 years old who work on themselves, and they go back and touch wounds from 50, 40, 60, 30 years ago. So you have the ability to do anything if you can put your mind to it. So it's never too late.
>
> And here's the deal, get rid of time anyway. It's not about how late is it or how old I'm at. How about this? While I'm here, I might as well figure out as much as I can about myself. I might as well seek to understand myself as much as I can. Make that your intention. And then when the clock's over and the game's over, you gotta take off this skin, you can look back on your life and say, wow, I knew myself better at this age than I did at this age.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 拜託聽我說這句話，謝謝。改變你的心態和頻率，永遠不嫌晚。人家說老狗學不會新把戲，那是騙人的。老狗學得會，只要你夠堅持、夠有意識、夠有韌性、夠有毅力，願意去做困難的事，願意回頭面對你一直掃到地毯下面的那些人生片段。
>
> 我看過六、七十歲的人在做自我成長，回頭去碰五十年、四十年、六十年、三十年前的傷口。所以只要你下定決心，什麼都做得到。永遠不嫌晚。
>
> 而且說真的，乾脆把時間這件事丟掉。重點不是現在多晚、我幾歲了。不如這樣想：既然我人還在這裡，就盡量把自己搞懂，盡量去理解自己。把這個當成你的目標。等到時間到了、比賽結束、你要脫下這副皮囊的時候，你回頭看自己的人生可以說：哇，我在這個年紀比在那個年紀更了解自己。
