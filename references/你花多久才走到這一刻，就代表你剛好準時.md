# 你花多久才走到這一刻，就代表你剛好準時

- 文案：It's never "too late" to start over 🫶 #nevertoolate #positivequotes #positivity

## 口白（原文）

> But I can tell you one thing, everybody that I know is going through some sort of reinvention. One of my friends just ended her corporate career and she's moving back to her hometown to go back to uni, and she's 35. Another one of my friends is starting over building a business, and she's 33. Another friend is 40 and she just left her job and moved abroad to an art residency. I left my tech career at 32.
>
> And if you feel like you're behind in life, let me tell you one thing. However long it took you to arrive to this moment, to realize that you want to change things, you want to move, you want to start over, you want to end that relationship because it's not working for you, this means that you are right on time, because the time is now. It doesn't matter how old you are if you choose yourself now.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 但我可以告訴你一件事：我認識的每一個人，都在經歷某種形式的重新開始。我有個朋友剛結束她的上班族生涯，準備搬回老家重新讀大學，她 35 歲。另一個朋友正在從頭開始創業，她 33 歲。還有一個朋友 40 歲，剛辭職搬到國外去做藝術駐村。我自己 32 歲離開科技業。
>
> 如果你覺得自己的人生落後了，讓我告訴你一件事。不管你花了多久才走到這一刻，才意識到你想改變、想搬家、想重新開始、想結束那段對你沒好處的關係，這都代表你剛好準時，因為時間就是現在。只要你現在選擇自己，幾歲都不重要。
