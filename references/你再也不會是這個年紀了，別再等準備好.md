## 口白（原文）

> You will never be this age again. So please do what makes you happy. Stop waiting for permission. Stop waiting for the right time. Stop waiting to feel ready.
>
> Take the trip. Start the thing. Tell the person. Wear the outfit. Try the new hobby.
>
> Life is happening right now. Not someday. Not when you're more prepared. Right now. And the older you get, the more you'll wish you could have cared less about what people thought, and more about what made you feel alive.
>
> So just do it. Be happy. Live fully. Because you only get one shot at being this version of yourself.

## 口白（繁體中文）

> 你再也不會是這個年紀了。所以拜託，做讓你快樂的事。別再等別人准許。別再等對的時機。別再等自己準備好。
>
> 去旅行。開始那件事。跟那個人說。穿那套衣服。試那個新嗜好。
>
> 人生就在此刻發生。不是哪一天，不是等你更有準備的時候。就是現在。而且你年紀越大，越會希望當初少在乎一點別人怎麼想，多在乎一點什麼讓你覺得活著。
>
> 所以就去做吧。開心一點。活得完整。因為這個版本的你，只有一次機會。
